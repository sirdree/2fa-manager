☁️ Chrome Sync

Sync your encrypted vault across all Chrome browsers signed into your Google account. No configuration required!

✨ How it works:
  • Uses Chrome's built-in sync (same as bookmarks, passwords)
  • Your vault remains encrypted with your master password
  • Automatically syncs across devices signed into your Google account
  • Uses Chrome Sync Storage with limited size - see limits
